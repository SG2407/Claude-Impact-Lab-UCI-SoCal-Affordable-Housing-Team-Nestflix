# Judging

## How it runs

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 2:30 PM | Submissions close. Judges shortlist from all submissions |
| 3:00 PM | Finalists announced. They present live in the auditorium |
| 4:00 PM | Judges deliberate |
| 4:15 PM | Winners and photos |

Keep your project running and ready to share either way.

## How winners are picked

Judges score each finalist from 1 to 5 in four areas. The four areas have equal weight.

| Area | The question |
|---|---|
| **Impact** | Would a real nonprofit or city use this next month? |
| **Works** | Does the live demo work? Working beats polished |
| **Rigor** | Are sources and assumptions shown? Are made-up numbers labelled? |
| **Creativity** | How new and useful is the idea? |

A perfect project scores 5 in all four.

## Prizes

Overall 1st, 2nd and 3rd, plus a winner in each lane.

## What finalists need

A live demo is required for finalists. Slides alone do not place.

## What helps

- One named user, with a clear before and after.
- A tool that works on a real, public case, such as a publicly listed small building or lot in
  Orange County.
- Sources on screen. Assumptions on screen. "Unknown" where it is unknown.
- Honesty about what is mocked and where the tool is wrong.

## What does not place

- Slides without a working demo.
- Made-up numbers presented as facts.
- Anything built on real personal information.
